## Decision

Lead with a complete menswear look in cool-gray architecture. Keep the black half-zip collar construction visible in the original reference; treat the generated full look as editorial concept imagery. Preserve the original files and exact Version 2 V icon geometry. Use one model, one garment, one location and consistent soft daylight for the new shoot. Existing imagery does not independently verify manufactured garments, materials, ownership or photographic origin.

## Type, rhythm, palette and crop recommendations

- Retain a refined serif such as the existing Cormorant Garamond, with weight 400 for headings and readable body; weight 300 only for large brand display. Keep VALDOVIERI spelling intact. Reduce normal headings' letter spacing to near zero and display wordmark tracking from `.51em` toward `.20–.28em` on desktop, `.14–.22em` on small screens after visual fit checks.
- Current product material copy is **8.96px**, footer contact **8.64px**, request label **9.6px** at 390px wide. Raise metadata/navigation/contact to 12–13px and body to 16–18px. Restrict spaced mono uppercase to short labels; use plain sentence case for descriptive copy. Preserve serif distinction with a quiet readable sans for UI if desired.
- Use black `#101113`, charcoal `#202226`, slate `#757B82`, soft gray `#D8DBDE`, white `#F4F5F6`, and small navy `#202936` accents. Replace current brown-gray muted text; no cream field, gold, ornamental rules or gilded imagery. The existing camel accessory can remain secondary.
- Hero landscape 16:9/3:2 and mobile portrait need separate considered framing, ideally same scene. Keep complete head-to-shoes silhouette, at least 5–8% breathing space around head/feet, shoulder seam/hem visible, and text outside key construction. Do not rely on heavy vignetting for readability. Prefer adjacent or lower-band text if no clean image zone.
- Transition from hero into a readable introduction and one complementary worn-outerwear frame, then preserved collection images, then clear inquiry. On mobile use 24px side gutters, bounded image blocks, and roughly 56–80px between editorial sections. Avoid three repeated 88svh image chapters.
- Product imagery should retain crowns, collar and zipper endpoints; cap brim must remain intact. For the original dark top, do not overcrop or digitally reconstruct unseen hem. It can remain a smaller supplied reference while the full-look concept is explicitly distinguished in provenance.

## Claims requiring evidence or removal

“Italian melton wool,” “ribbed merino,” “brushed black wool,” “wool twill,” “satin lining,” and implied longevity in “cut for the long term” are not verified by photos or repository records. “Two-way metal zip” conflicts with the visible single chest-length closure. “Embossed leather V” conflicts with apparent stitching. Avoid introducing country of manufacture, mill, handwork, limited availability, pricing, delivery or production-status claims. “Private access” should only describe a functioning request experience, not imply confirmed stock/exclusivity. “Found only at close range” is poorly supported by the current duplicate beanie frame and large cap emblem.

Safe copy can name visible form without material assertion: “A study in dark tones,” “Half-zip silhouette,” “Ribbed headwear,” “Tonal cap,” “Collection imagery,” and “Inquire about the collection.” Generated assets must have explicit records such as `ai-generated-editorial-concept`, preserve exact prompts/references, and never be labeled verified product photography or manufactured samples.

## Cohesive replacement shoot brief

Priority A: complete menswear look. Adult male in the reference's black matte high-neck HALF-ZIP top, charcoal straight trousers and simple black shoes. Natural upright posture with gentle shoulder/hip support against a cool-gray architectural pier or low ledge, credible feet on ground, calm gaze slightly off camera, hands anatomically clear and relaxed. No theatrical lean, contorted wrists or fashion grimace. No cap in hero; any later headwear is low profile and black. Soft overcast directional daylight with gentle fill; moderate contrast revealing garment folds, collar, zip endpoint, cuff and hem. Same model/wardrobe/location for portrait companion. 35–50mm equivalent optics, realistic textile gravity, cool neutral grade, quiet architecture with restrained negative space. Avoid borrowed campaign signatures.

Priority B: complementary 4:5/3:2 worn garment frame from same shoot. Waist-to-head or three-quarter framing with both shoulder joins, coherent collar curves, single zipper/teeth/placket and relaxed sleeve folds visible. Human skin pores, natural fingers, plausible ear/eye details. Do not change garment hardware or add leather badges/pockets.

Existing headwear/cap/texture images remain useful secondary collection references. New supporting imagery should not be unrelated mannequin/products, cream still-life objects, generic luxury props, or ornamental architecture. Check at full resolution for skin/hands, fabric weave, stitch continuity, closures, gravity, contact shadows, perspective and proportional garment fit. Reject defects rather than darkening them. Keep generated concept provenance explicit and supplied originals immutable.
