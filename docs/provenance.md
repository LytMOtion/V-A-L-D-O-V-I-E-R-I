# Product round provenance and claims

This round replaces the rejected half-zip/cap presentation with three new generated design concepts. It does not establish manufactured products. Existing original assets and rejected-round evidence are preserved.

Fifteen distinct photographs are generated using the approved built-in image generation tool: six front/detail pairs and three separate editorial scenes. Source/served-file SHA256 and dimensions are in product-image-provenance.json. Prompt records are in product-image-prompts. The image-production specialist completed asset creation but its final reporting stream disconnected; the integration owner completed the deployed-file manifest. Existing partial tool lineage remains in the local evidence checkpoint; absent historical output mappings are not fabricated.

The exact supplied Version 2 icon path is used unchanged in all views. Three generated photographic thread fields are clipped inside that native vector, with approximate scale, landmark placement and camera rotation recorded in data/mark-placements.json. This is a digital tonal-thread design study, not verified physical embroidery. Earlier matte-print tests are not used. Visible glyph widths 10/16/12mm for jacket/cap/beanie are proposals, not measured samples.

Colors are proposed: jacket/cap Graphite and Soft Gray; beanie Graphite and Midnight Navy. They are not owner-approved production colors. Swatches navigate to separate matching front/detail images. No full-photo color filters or fake availability are used.

Open candidate-image findings: Selected Soft Gray jacket front twill v3/detail twill v4 improve chest/body directional yarns, but curling/ring grain remains in collar, neck, shoulder and sleeve regions; Soft Gray cap weave scale/local curling remains inconsistent with Graphite. Cap Graphite native thread detail provisionally passed personal material review. All three specialists personally passed both Beanie v11 native detail proofs as generated tonal-thread concepts: fine photographic strands remain distinct while actual knit crest/valley shading continues. This is a visual concept pass, not stitch or manufacture certification. Jacket native placement/contact is provisional at its small 41px glyph. Final paired fronts, editorial marks, mobile crops and site behavior require deployed review. Do not interpret a working Preview as owner approval.

Unsupported claims are removed or expressly unconfirmed: wool/cashmere/fiber percentages, Italian origin, manufacturing location or technique, handmade construction, fit measurements, inventory, pricing, delivery and availability. The copy describes visible proposed shapes only. Footer and product pages identify concept imagery and unconfirmed facts.

Canela and Söhne licensed assets were not present in the repository or scoped source files. Existing Cormorant Garamond and Inter are retained. No paid fonts or image licenses were bought. No external brand campaign was copied.
